🔔 Open Breakout — US market open (15:30 your time). The one intraday strategy that backtested with an edge. The rule, per stock: mark the high/low of the first 15 minutes after the 9:30 ET open, then take a break of that range only in the direction of the 200-EMA trend (counter-trend breaks had no edge). Stop = other side of the range; target = nearest daily pivot (S/R).
⚠ 60-day sample only — promising, not proven. Costs here are modelled light; on real CFDs the open is the widest-spread moment, which could eat the edge. Forward-test on demo before real funds. Not financial advice — you decide and own the risk.
